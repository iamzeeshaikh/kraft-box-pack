# Product audit

All 162 product URLs, classified against twelve months of Search Console
data. No page is merged, noindexed or redirected on low traffic alone — the
brief rules that out and the data supports it: several pages with almost no
impressions rank in the top five for narrow terms.

## Decisions

| Status | Products | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Priority commercial | 34 | 2,000+ impressions or 15+ clicks. Hand-written metadata, content rewritten first. |
| Standard product | 38 | 500–2,000 impressions. Content rewritten. |
| Supporting niche | 86 | Under 500 impressions. Content rewritten; kept for long-tail terms and link equity. |
| Recreated | 4 | Dropped by the migration, still ranking, rebuilt as full pages. **No photography — excluded from the Merchant feed.** |
| Merge candidate | 0 | None. No two products serve the same intent closely enough. |
| Noindex candidate | 0 | None. Every page is unique content with a commercial purpose. |
| Redirect candidate | 0 | None among live products. Five *lost* URLs were redirected — see MIGRATION-RECONCILIATION.md. |

**All 162 have hand-written content**: an intro, sections chosen for that
product, one contextual outbound link and ten product-specific FAQs. Product
body similarity above 20% is **0 pairs**.

## Why nothing was merged or noindexed

The obvious merge candidates look like duplicates by name and are not by intent.
`kraft-shipping-boxes` and `kraft-mailer-boxes` are different constructions for
different journeys. `small-kraft-boxes` and `mini-kraft-boxes` differ at the
point where machine gluing stops being possible. `kraft-rectangular-gift-boxes`
and `kraft-square-gift-boxes` fill differently and suit different contents.

Each now says so on its own page, which is a better answer than merging them.
